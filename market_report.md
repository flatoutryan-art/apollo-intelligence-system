# Apollo Africa: Competitive Intelligence Analysis

## 3 Key Business Observations

---

### **1. REGULATORY UNCERTAINTY IS CREATING A "HAVES vs. HAVE-NOTS" MARKET SPLIT**

**The Signal:**
Apollo's national trading licence is **suspended as of July 2025** pending finalisation of electricity trading rules (due June 2026). Meanwhile, competitors licensed *before* the October 2024 Eskom challenge (NOA Group, Enpower Trading, Etana Energy, PowerX) are operating freely with active national licences.

**Market Impact:**
- **Green-light competitors** (PowerX, Enpower, Etana, NOA) can scale nationally while Apollo waits
- The licence suspension has created a 9-month operational handicap for Apollo and 3 other challenged traders
- **Lyra Energy, Investec Trading, BluEnergy, Neura Trading** licensed post-suspension are now competing without the shadow of Eskom's legal challenge
- This creates a critical timing risk: by the time trading rules finalize in June 2026, competitors will have secured long-term PPA relationships and customer lock-in

**Apollo's Position:**
Apollo is in the weakest regulatory posture of its peer set—challenged, suspended, and dependent on NERSA's June 2026 rule publication. This is a **structural disadvantage in customer acquisition**.

---

### **2. MUNICIPAL NETWORK ACCESS IS BECOMING THE REAL COMPETITIVE MOAT (NOT NATIONAL LICENCES)**

**The Signal:**
The data reveals a hidden geography of market control. Only a handful of competitors have signed Use-of-System Agreements (UoSAs) with municipalities:
- **Active UoSAs:** Cape Town (City Power), Nelson Mandela Bay, George, Overstrand
- **In discussion:** Johannesburg / City Power, Ekurhuleni
- **New entrant:** BluEnergy (municipal-focused strategy; Feb 2026 licence)
- **Potential scale play:** Cigicell received licence for 95+ municipalities (Feb 2026) but UoSAs not yet signed

**Market Impact:**
- A trader with a UoSA can serve ALL C&I customers in that municipality without using Eskom's constrained grid
- **Eskom grid = bottleneck.** **Municipal networks = scalability.**
- The traders winning in 2025-26 are those quietly signing UoSAs with metros (Johannesburg, Durban, Ekurhuleni)
- This is a 12-18 month headstart game: whoever secures UoSAs in the Big 3 metros (Joburg, Durban, Ekurhuleni) first will own 60%+ of the C&I market

**Apollo's Position:**
Apollo has UoSAs in **4 smaller municipalities** (Cape Town, Nelson Mandela Bay, George, Overstrand). The intelligence shows Apollo is "in discussion" with Johannesburg City Power but **not yet live**. Meanwhile, competitors are moving faster on municipal expansion.

### **3. SAWEM LAUNCH (JUNE 2026) WILL SEPARATE TECHNOLOGY-READY TRADERS FROM THE REST**

**The Signal:**
Only **GreenCo Power Services** has a documented SAWEM-ready ETRM system. Apollo's SAWEM readiness is **"UNKNOWN"** and "not confirmed anywhere" in public sources. The data shows:

| Competitor | Tech Capability | SAWEM Status |
|---|---|---|
| **GreenCo** | Full ETRM | Yes – SAPP-ready |
| **NOA Group** | Proprietary platform | In development |
| **Neura Trading** | Proprietary (SA-specific) | Partial – needs SAWEM module |
| **Apollo Africa** | Not disclosed | UNKNOWN |
| **Discovery Green / Ampli** | Not disclosed | Challenged + suspended |

**Market Impact:**
- SAWEM Phase 1 launches **1 June 2026**—8 weeks away from the data date
- Traders without real-time ETRM systems cannot:
  - Participate in day-ahead market auctions
  - Manage multi-counterparty settlement
  - Hedge trading risk in real time
  - Scale beyond bilateral
